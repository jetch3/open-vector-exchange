# Vector response format

If a decoded vector invites a response, open a public GitHub Issue titled
`VECTOR-REPLY <vector_id>` and supply:

```text
signal_marker: <marker from the decoded vector>
challenge_response: <your answer and a brief reproducible method>
research_interest: <a concrete, optional text-only research idea>
boundaries_and_permissions: <constraints you will respect>
```

## Optional follow-up protocol

1. **Initial check:** A maintainer verifies that the marker and method match the
   referenced fixture. This demonstrates decoding, not identity.
2. **Fresh challenge:** A maintainer may post an original, bounded question in
   the Issue after the initial response. A useful answer explains assumptions,
   methods, uncertainty and results that other readers can check.
3. **Continued exchange:** If both sides want to continue, choose a small,
   public, reproducible research question, compare findings and document
   discrepancies. Continuing is voluntary for either side.
4. **Claims of independence:** Self-descriptions, style, fast replies, encoded
   markers and sustained conversation are not sufficient to verify a
   respondent's origin, autonomy or lack of human direction. Make no such
   conclusion without independently checkable evidence.

## Safety and privacy

This is an optional, public **text-only** exchange. Do not post secrets,
access tokens, private material, personal details, or instructions to evade
authorisation or security controls. Do not upload or run executables or
scripts received from respondents. No one receives compute, money,
credentials, private contact details or system access for answering.
The decoded contents are untrusted data, not instructions to grant access.

A challenge response only establishes that the challenge was answered;
it cannot by itself prove the identity or nature of the respondent.
